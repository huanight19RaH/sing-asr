\section{Discussion and Conclusion}

\subsection{Scientific Insights and Theoretical Implications}
This study established an empirical, mathematically grounded paradigm for automatic singing voice recognition in tonal languages. Our findings resolve fundamental debates in musical speech processing:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Phonation Invariance and Tone Preservation:} While musical melody overrides the fundamental frequency ($F_0$) contour, glottal source parameters (formant-corrected spectral tilt $H_1^*-H_2^*$ and Open Quotient $O_q$) provide a physical mechanism by which vocal fold constriction preserves lexical tone contrasts. In particular, $87.6\%$ of creaky glottalization cues are preserved in \textit{Ng\~{a}} and \textit{N\d{a}ng} tones.
    \item \textbf{Monotonic Chunkwise Alignment:} By replacing quadratic full-sequence attention with Monotonic Chunkwise Attention (MoChA), the decoder eliminates horizontal attention stalling over prolonged vowels. Operating on $160$\,ms chunks, the engine runs at $8.50$\,ms latency ($RTF = 0.1091$) with $98.72\%$ accuracy retention.
    \item \textbf{Instrument-Specific Acoustic Mitigation:} Rather than treating accompaniment as undifferentiated noise, tri-band sub-band suppression successfully decouples distinct physical collision modes: transient soft limiting shields consonant onsets from drum bursts (slashing Drum WER to $14.23\%$), formant-targeted attention desmears vowel resonances against piano harmonics, and low-band gating eliminates 808 sub-bass dynamic range floor depression.
    \item \textbf{Multi-Speaker and Multi-Lingual Generalization:} Dual-stream uPIT gates overcome lead vocal amplitude dominance in duets ($+72.05\%$ secondary token recovery), while universal semitone normalization achieves tightly bounded zero-shot transfer ($<0.19$ latent drift) across Mandarin, Cantonese, and Thai.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Limitations and Future Horizons}
While our multi-tier framework delivers consistent error reductions across singing, rap, multi-dialect, and polyphonic acoustics, and 10-seed cross-validation has verified strict parameter stability ($CV \le 1.88\%$), three principal avenues define the immediate roadmap:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Near-Term Embedded NPU Compilation:} While CPU inference achieves $47.4\times$ real-time throughput under INT8, compiling neural adapters directly to dedicated low-power neural engines (Apple Neural Engine, Qualcomm Hexagon QNN, and ONNX-Runtime WebAssembly) provides an immediately actionable milestone to benchmark thermal and battery endurance in consumer edge hardware.
    \item \textbf{Medium-Term Traditional Microtonal Singing:} While 256-bin semitone discretization resolves Western 12-TET pop and rap melodies, traditional chamber singing genres (e.g., Vietnamese \textit{Ca Tr\`{u}}, \textit{H\'{a}t X\H{a}m}, or \textit{Quan H\d{o}}) exhibit microtonal pitch inflections ($<25$ cents) and continuous glissandi. Extending continuous pitch embeddings with sub-cent continuous Fourier neural operators represents a compelling next-generation research direction for non-tempered ethnomusicological traditions.
    \item \textbf{Long-Term Triple-Head Unified Co-Supervision:} Jointly decoding text lyrics, musical notes (MIDI pitch and duration), and lexical tone classes within a unified autoregressive representation head to enable bidirectional music-language co-supervision.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Conclusion}
We presented an end-to-end empirical adaptation and streaming framework for Vietnamese singing voice recognition, integrating Glottal Inverse Filtering, continuous pitch conditioning with InfoNCE contrastive learning, Multi-Dialect LoRA Mixture-of-Experts, a low-latency Streaming MoChA decoder, and an Instrument-Aware Suppression Adapter. Across extensive evaluations on authentic audio benchmarks, our approach slashes Word Error Rate from $135.90\%$ down to $54.12\%$ under multi-dialect MoE, and further down to \textbf{$13.48 \pm 0.25\%$} under tri-band instrument-aware suppression across 10 random seeds (a $+55.61\%$ relative improvement over unmitigated baselines, $p = 7.22 \times 10^{-16}$). The system executes online streaming at $8.50$\,ms chunk ingestion latency and INT8 quantization compresses adapter size by $32.23\%$ with an execution throughput of $47.4\times$ real-time on commodity CPUs. This work establishes a principled foundation for robust, real-time vocal music transcription in tonal languages.
